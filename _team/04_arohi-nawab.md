Arohi Nawab is a senior at UC San Diego studying Economics with a minor in Business Analytics. At the Power Transformation Lab, she contributes to research examining the effects of US industrial and trade policies on clean energy manufacturing. Her work involves collecting and consolidating policy, trade, tariff, and economic data to support econometric analysis. She is interested in the intersection of applied economics, international trade, and energy policy. Arohi hopes to apply data-driven research methods to understand how public policy shapes markets and economic outcomes.
