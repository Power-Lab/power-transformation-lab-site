Wenshuo Liu is a second-year undergraduate at UC San Diego majoring in Mathematics–Economics. At the Power Transformation Lab, Wenshuo works on collecting, cleaning, and mapping data on China's electricity markets and helps maintain the project website. Wenshuo is interested in applying econometrics and data analysis to energy markets and policy. Before joining the lab, Wenshuo interned in Beijing. Outside of the lab, Wenshuo enjoys golf, falconry, and reading.
