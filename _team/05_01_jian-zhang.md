Jian is an Associate Professor in the School of Economics and Management at North China Electric Power University and a Visiting Scholar in the School of Global Policy and Strategy at the University of California, San Diego for 2026-2027. His research focuses on energy transition policies and the long-term evolution of China's new power system. Currently, he is investigating the impact of climate risks on the reliability and economic efficiency of power systems, such as generation expansion planning, resilience assessment, and price risk evaluation. Jian holds a Ph.D. and a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from Beijing Jiaotong University and subsequently conducted two years of postdoctoral research at the Department of Electrical Engineering at Tsinghua University. He has been on the faculty at North China Electric Power University since 2020.
