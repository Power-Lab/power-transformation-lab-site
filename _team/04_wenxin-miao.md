Wenxin Miao is a second-year undergraduate student majoring in Cognitive Science with a Specialization in Design & Interaction at UC San Diego. At the Power Transformation Lab, she works on collecting, organizing and processing data related to China’s electricity markets and energy systems, and she is also responsible for maintaining project websites. She is passionate about leveraging human-centered and adaptive design to better convey complex information from energy systems to distinct groups. In her free time, Miao enjoys singing, roller skating, traveling, photography, and watching movies.
