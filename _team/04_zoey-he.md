Zoey is a senior undergraduate student at UC San Diego majoring in Data Science. At the Power Transformation Lab, she works on energy data processing and validation for large-scale generator and capacity datasets across Chinese electricity markets, supporting research on power system optimization and energy transitions. She is interested in applying data-driven methods to improve the reliability of large-scale energy models. In her free time, Zoey enjoys photography, oil pastel drawing, and spending time alone.
