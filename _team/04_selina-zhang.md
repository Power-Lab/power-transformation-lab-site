Selina Zhang is a third-year undergraduate majoring in Data Science at UC San Diego. At the Power Transformation Lab, she focuses on Southeast Asia’s energy systems, electricity consumption, and regional power transformation trends. Her work involves collecting, cleaning, and analyzing large-scale energy and economic datasets to support research on sustainable development and energy policy. She is particularly interested in applying her data science skills to real-world challenges in energy transition, infrastructure planning, and environmental sustainability. In her free time, she enjoys exploring cafés, traveling, listening to music, and learning about different cultures.
